## Services

<div class="feature-grid">
  <div class="feature">
    <div class="ico">🔥</div>
    <h3>Forensic electrical &amp; fire investigation</h3>
    <p>Origin-and-cause investigation of fires with an electrical or battery component, following NFPA 921: site survey, arc mapping, evidence preservation, metallurgical and microscopy examination of conductors, and independent reports for courts and authorities.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature">
    <div class="ico">🔋</div>
    <h3>Lithium-ion battery incidents</h3>
    <p>Thermal-runaway assessment of consumer and cargo-related battery products, including cell-level failure analysis and review of transport and dangerous-goods requirements.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature">
    <div class="ico">🧪</div>
    <h3>Product testing &amp; reliability</h3>
    <p>Testing of cables, transformers, lighting systems, fans, motors, solar panels and other electrical components, and design review of drive electronics (for example BLDC fan controllers) for warranty-grade reliability.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature">
    <div class="ico">⚖️</div>
    <h3>Regulatory &amp; technical committees</h3>
    <p>Technical assessment of manufacturing facilities and equipment claims for regulators, and expert membership of government committees.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature">
    <div class="ico">🏗️</div>

## Selected engagements

| Client | Nature of work |
|---|---|
| **Civil Aviation Authority of Bangladesh (CAAB)** | Independent forensic engineering investigation for the authority. |
| **Jatiya Sangsad Secretariat** | Independent origin-and-cause examination of a fire, including laboratory examination of evidence. |
| **Ministry of Disaster Management** | Led a committee report on lightning-strike trends in Bangladesh. |
| **National Board of Revenue (NBR)** | Technical expert on an inspection committee, representing BUET EEE. |
| **Jamuna Electronics** | Reliability review of BLDC ceiling-fan drive electronics (possible student projects: reverse-engineering the controller board and identifying its components; thermal and accelerated-life testing of the drive to support the warranty period; efficiency and EMI measurements of the drive). |
| **Adex** | Automatic voltage regulator (AVR) work (possible student projects: AVR modelling and controller tuning; bench testing of the regulator against generator excitation requirements; transient-response and stability studies).
| **CPGC, WASA, BWDB, BTRC, BIWTA, DESCO, DPDC, LP Gas** | Recruitment examinations for engineering posts, run through BRTC. |
