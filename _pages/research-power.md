## Research directions

<div class="feature-grid">
  <div class="feature">
    <div class="ico">🚗</div>
    <h3>Wireless charging of electric vehicles</h3>
    <p>Resonant inductive power transfer for EVs with LCC-LCC compensation: coil design, disturbance analysis of uncoordinated and coordinated source-transition methods in PV/grid interchangeable systems, and charging-grid interaction.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature">
    <div class="ico">⚙️</div>
    <h3>Drive electronics and reliability</h3>
    <p>Reliability-driven design of BLDC motor drives, with attention to warranty and field-failure behaviour (see <a href="{{ '/consultancy/' | relative_url }}">consultancy</a>).</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature">
    <div class="ico">☀️</div>
    <h3>Solar PV in regional grids</h3>
    <p>Optimal allocation of utility-scale photovoltaic plants in Bangladesh's regional transmission grid, using particle-swarm optimisation with static and time-series assessment.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature">
    <div class="ico">🎛️</div>
    <h3>Automatic voltage regulators (AVR)</h3>
    <p>Design, modelling and tuning of automatic voltage regulators for synchronous generators: excitation-system models, controller tuning, transient response and stability of the regulated terminal voltage.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature">
    <div class="ico">📈</div>
    <h3>Grid simulation and ML-based derating prediction</h3>
    <p>Power-system simulation of Bangladesh's grid, combined with machine-learning algorithms that predict the derating of power plants (the loss of available capacity under temperature, fuel and operating conditions) to support planning and dispatch.</p>
  </div>
</div>
