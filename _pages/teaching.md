## Thesis supervision

I supervise B.Sc. (EEE 400) and M.Sc. theses. Recent topics include topological-insulator transistors, borophene and black-phosphorus devices, thermoelectrics, ferroelectric neuromorphic devices and power-electronics systems. I also serve on examination boards for theses in nanoelectronics, photonics, energy systems and digital design.

## Accreditation and curriculum

I contribute to outcome-based education (OBE) and BAETE accreditation work in the department, in the context of the Washington Accord.

## Laboratory oversight

High Voltage Laboratory, Power Electronics Laboratory and the EV Research Laboratory at the 4IR Park.
