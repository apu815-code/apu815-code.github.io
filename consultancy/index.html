consultancy

Independent engineering investigation, testing and expert advice for government, industry and utilities, delivered through BUET's Bureau of Research, Testing and Consultation (BRTC).

I take on engineering work that draws on both research-level physics and hands-on electrical practice. Engagements are carried out through the Bureau of Research, Testing and Consultation (BRTC), BUET and follow its procedures for independence, documentation and confidentiality.

Services

🔥

Forensic electrical & fire investigation

Origin-and-cause investigation of fires with an electrical or battery component, following NFPA 921: site survey, arc mapping, evidence preservation, metallurgical and microscopy examination of conductors, and independent reports for courts and authorities.

🔋

Lithium-ion battery incidents

Thermal-runaway assessment of consumer and cargo-related battery products, including cell-level failure analysis and review of transport and dangerous-goods requirements.

🧪

Product testing & reliability

Testing of cables, transformers, lighting systems, fans, motors, solar panels and other electrical components, and design review of drive electronics (for example BLDC fan controllers) for warranty-grade reliability.

⚖️

Regulatory & technical committees

Technical assessment of manufacturing facilities and equipment claims for regulators, and expert membership of government committees.

🏗️

Electrical design

Electrical system design for large residential complexes, educational institutions, university dormitories and port facilities.

📝

Recruitment examinations

Question-setting and evaluation for technical posts across public utilities and organizations, coordinated with senior colleagues at BRTC.
